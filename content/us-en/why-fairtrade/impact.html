Fairtrade’s impact

When you see more and more product labels in the supermarket, it’s not always easy to understand what they mean. Through the following resources, you can find out about the difference Fairtrade makes for farmers and workers. From data and research, to programme results, and stories from Fairtrade producers, we highlight the impact Fairtrade has on their lives, businesses, and working conditions.
