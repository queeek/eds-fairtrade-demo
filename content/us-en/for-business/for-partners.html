Frequently Asked Questions

I need quick content for one of our digital channels. Where do I find that?

First check out our resource hub above for toolkits, photos and framework guidance. Our Marketing the Movement page provides examples of how to put these into practice. Then head over to Impact Stories and our Instagram for inspiration or commodity pages for quick stats.

I want to get more products certified. Who should I talk to?

Amazing! Please reach out to license@fairtradeamerica.org.

I am working on updating our packaging with the Fairtrade Mark. How do I do that?

You can submit your updated artwork and recipes for review through our digital portal—Connect. Visit the Mark Guidelines as a refresher as well. For any questions, please reach out to your account manager.
