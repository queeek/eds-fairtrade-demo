What is Fairtrade?

Fairtrade is the most recognised and trusted sustainability label working to make trade fairer for the people behind the products sold and consumed around the world.

At the heart of Fairtrade are social, economic and environmental standards designed to create fairer trading conditions and support more sustainable production. Farmers and workers are actively involved in shaping the Fairtrade system and its standards.

Fairtrade for your business

Whether you manufacture products, build a purpose-led brand or manage a retail portfolio, Fairtrade offers ready solutions for companies of all sizes to manage risks in their supply chains.

The Fairtrade mark on your product shows that you are partnering with Fairtrade. It indicates that the product meets relevant criteria of the Fairtrade standards and that, together with us, you are working to improve living and working conditions in producing countries. The Fairtrade mark can help you build trust, differentiate your products and reach new target audiences. Fairtrade supports partners with marketing initiatives and campaigns.

Product certification

If you manufacture or package products yourself, an external audit is required. This applies up to the point in the supply chain where the products are fully packaged for consumers. The audit is conducted by FLOCERT, an external and independent certification body as required by law. FLOCERT verifies that retailers comply with Fairtrade standards.

If you buy finished products in consumer ready packaging from a certified manufacturer, you do not need certification, but a license agreement.

License Agreement

Anyone who wishes to sell products bearing the Fairtrade mark under its own brand or company name must have a license agreement with Fairtrade Germany.

The standardized license agreement ensures the comparability, transparency, and credibility of the Fairtrade mark. The agreement covers the following:

  • Permission to use the Fairtrade mark
  • Guidelines that must be followed, including certification and product design requirements
  • License fees

If you manufacture private label products for retail or discount, please contact us. We will advise you on the applicable contractual set up.

Once the license agreement has been signed, you will become a Fairtrade partner.

Registration and approval of labelled products

All products bearing a Fairtrade Mark must be registered and approved in the online platform CONNECT. When the product has been approved in CONNECT, you can start selling and promoting your Fairtrade products.

Recognition and marketing

By using our mark, you can communicate your commitment to Fairtrade and build on the positive associations consumers have with the mark.

Fairtrade beyond the mark

Who we are

For more than three decades, Fairtrade has been making an impact on the way trade works. We believe that every farmer and worker should have access to a better way of doing business, and a better way of living.

As a leader in the global movement to make trade fair, Fairtrade supports and challenges businesses and governments and connects farmers and workers with the people who buy their products.

What we do

Fairtrade changes the way trade works through better prices, decent working conditions and a fairer deal for farmers and workers. 
We offer retail and industrial partners as well as consumers concrete alternatives for action, so that farming families and workers in countries of the Global South can have better prospects for the future.

Why we do it

We want to build a world where everyone benefits from quality products and enjoys quality of life. But this cannot be done in a silo. You cannot separate human rights from environmental rights, child protection from living incomes, and gender equity from worker’s rights, amongst others. Our holistic approach looks from different dimensions to drive impact – and fairness.
