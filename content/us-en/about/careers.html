A Different Kind of Team Culture

At Fairtrade, we care not only about what we do but also how we do it. We make an intentional effort to continuously learn how to work more effectively to reach our collective goal of uplifting the lives of farmers and workers. As a team, this looks like a lot of collaboration, a lot of supporting one another, and practicing the listening muscle so we can hear one anothers' feedback as we listen to the needs of our farmer and farming partners.

How We Work

Optimism

We recognize the reality of unjust systems and also see the possibilities in the problems we confront every day. We pursue our work with a dogged belief that we can and must contribute to a better future.

Sustainability

As individuals and as an organization, we are committed to making decisions today that support a vibrant future. We prioritize taking care of ourselves and our teams so that we have the verve to do the work ahead. We work towards long-term solutions that build generational security and foster environmental resilience.

Curiosity

We leverage the intersection of our wonder and humility to further the Fairtrade mission. We believe that taking calculated risks is an essential part of personal and organizational growth. By intentionally creating a culture where failing forward is welcome, we give ourselves space to ask big questions, try new things and learn from our collective experiences.

Trust

We have confidence in the talents, efforts and intentions of each member of our team. We foster an environment of integrity through honest and transparent collaboration both as thoughtful leaders and active followers.

Empathy

We believe in showing up for people where they are—from holding space for another to providing tangible support. We leverage our seat of power in the US to actively include producers where decisions are being made about them without them.  Whatever form it takes, we’re committed to functionally honoring the dignity of all people.

Excellence

Excellence in our work is both the means and the ends in our pursuit of improving farmer livelihoods. We approach our work with a collective determination to raise the bar and encourage each other to push past roadblocks. As a result of this mentality we foster innovation, deliver tangible results and establish ourselves as a world-class partner uniquely positioned to create change.

We are Committed to Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

Fairtrade America recognizes that being inclusive is about action. Therefore, we are not only advocating for justice, equality and non-discrimination for farmers and workers in low- and middle-income countries, but also within our own organization and with commercial partners, governments and the communities where Fairtrade products are produced and purchased.

Fairtrade America is committed to an inclusive and diverse workforce. We do not discriminate on the basis of actual or perceived race, color, national origin, religion, age, sex or gender (including pregnancy, childbirth, or pregnancy-related conditions), gender identity or expression (including transgender status), marital status, sexual orientation, physical or mental disability, genetic information, military service or veteran status, or any other status protected by law.
