The bananas we love are under pressure

Climate change is bringing more extreme weather, diseases are threatening crops, and farming costs continue to rise.
The people who grow our bananas are already feeling the impact. Yet many struggle to earn enough to support their families or prepare for the challenges ahead.
Fairer business practices, stronger laws and better support for farming communities can help protect the future of bananas and build a food system that works for people and planet.

Do it Fair

When we listen to producers and farmers, what we hear is clear, fairness breaks down in three key places:

People - providing rights and safety at work
Planet - protecting the land producers depend on
Pay - earning enough to live well and plan for the future.

That’s why our 2026 focus is simple: People. Planet. Pay. Do it Fair.

What is Fairtrade?

Around the world, the people who grow, pick, and make the things we buy are facing the same challenges: unfair pay, unsafe working conditions, and a climate crisis threatening their livelihoods. Fairtrade exists to change this, and to push for the rules that make fairness the norm, not the exception.
