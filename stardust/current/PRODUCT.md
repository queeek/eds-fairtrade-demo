# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

_provenance: inferred — basis: the main navigation (Why Fairtrade · Products · For Business · Get Involved) and the per-country sites captured._

- Shoppers and citizens who want to understand the FAIRTRADE Mark and buy or campaign for fairer products ("Get Involved", "How to Shop Sustainably", "Where to buy").
- Businesses, brands and retailers considering certification or licensing ("For Business", "Get certified", "Benefits of being certified").
- Media and partners looking for facts, figures and contacts ("For Media", "Key figures at a glance").
- Visitors from many countries: the site is a network of national Fairtrade organisations, each with its own home page, logo and navigation (24 country/region home pages captured, in English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Swedish, Polish, Romanian, Slovenian).

## Product Purpose

The global web presence of the Fairtrade system: it explains what Fairtrade is and how certification works, shows the impact on farmers and workers, lists certified products, and routes businesses to certification and consumers to action — with a national site per Fairtrade organisation under one shared design system.

## Positioning

Fairtrade is the certification system behind the FAIRTRADE Mark, owned in part by the farmers and workers it serves ("+37,000 products sold with the Fairtrade Mark in more than 145 countries", "2M+ farmers and workers", "$2+ Billion in Fairtrade premium paid to farmers in the last decade" — home page number facts).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content-managed site (Adobe Experience Manager), one component library across every country site: page lead, hero carousel, number-fact teaser, text + image, rich text section, teaser grid, CTA section, accordion, single image, video, image grid, highlighted teaser carousel, embedded iframes and a form builder.
- Per-country header (logo, main navigation with desktop flyouts, search, language switcher) and footer.
- Consent management by Usercentrics on every page.
- `/` redirects to `/us-en.html` for the visitor captured (United States edition).

## Brand Commitments

- Register: `brand` (marketing / advocacy).
- Name: Fairtrade; country organisations keep their own names and logos (Fairtrade America, Max Havelaar France, Fairtrade Deutschland …).
- Personality observed: warm, optimistic, people-first; bold saturated colour (sky blue, flower pink, lime) with organic "wave" and blob shapes and large photography of producers.
- Voice observed: plain, direct, second person calls to action ("Get the Story Here!", "Find out more", "Read more").

## Product Principles

1. Producers first — farmers' and workers' stories and figures lead the content.
2. One system, many voices — every national organisation speaks in its own language on a shared design.
3. Credibility through evidence — numbers, standards and certification facts are stated plainly.
