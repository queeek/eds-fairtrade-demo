About Fairtrade NAPP

Building a secure, sustainable and resilient Network of Fairtrade Producers across Asia and the Pacific region!

Explore a diverse range of our products

Each product grown by our producers tells a story of tradition, hard work, and sustainable practices, reflecting the unique agricultural landscapes of their origins. From the lush, terraced hills of Southeast Asia to the diverse climatic zones of South Asia, from the sun-drenched fields of the Pacific Islands to the vast plains and varied climates of Central Asia, our geographic scope encompasses a remarkable array of landscapes and livelihoods. Here, smallholder farmers cultivate not just crops, but hope for a sustainable future.

Learn more

Areas of focus

Our vision is a world in which all producers can enjoy secure, sustainable livelihoods, fulfill their potential and decide on their future. But this cannot be done in silo. You cannot separate human rights from environmental rights, child protection from living incomes and gender equity from worker’s rights.

Key issues

Our Programs and services

We offer a unique blend of regional expertise and comprehensive services tailored to meet the diverse needs of producers across Asia and the Pacific region. Our commitment to fairtrade is reflected in our end-to-end support services, which empower farmers and workers at every stage of their journey.

Our geographic presence

At Fairtrade NAPP, we celebrate the vibrant tapestry of the Asia-Pacific region—a land where diverse cultures, rich traditions, and resilient communities intertwine. Our mission stretches across borders, uniting farmers and workers who are committed to fair trade principles.
