Fairtrade Certified Means Trust

The Fairtrade Mark is the most recognized ethical label in the world. Our network brings together nearly two million farmers and workers across the planet. Join our global community of brands, manufacturers and producers dedicated to making trade fair at every step of the supply chain.

Fairtrade is good for business

Shoppers are demanding more. They are more conscious than ever about fair wages, environmental impact, gender equality and human rights when they shop. Fairtrade Certification can give you insight into your supply chain and peace of mind, knowing that you did your due diligence to support the farmers and workers you source from with a fairer deal.

Fair trade is good for the world

Fairtrade Certification is a simple way to prioritize the people and places behind your products. By becoming Fairtrade Certified, you are leveraging your investment for a more sustainable future and joining an inspiring network of ethically-minded brands.

- Increases supply chain transparency
- Mandates workers' rights
- Amplifies sustainable farming practices
- Promotes gender equality

The Certification Process

More than 37,000 products carry the Fairtrade Mark worldwide. This label shows that each product meets our rigorous environmental, economic and social standards—and was audited by FLOCERT, an accredited third-party auditor.

The Fairtrade Standards are the foundation of our work. They establish what we mean by sustainable farming, decent work, and fair trading relations, and detail what we want to achieve.
