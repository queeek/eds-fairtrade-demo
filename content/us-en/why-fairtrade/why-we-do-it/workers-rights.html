Fairtrade stands - and acts - for workers' rights

In a world driven by consumer demands and global supply chains, workers are often left behind. Fairtrade works together with and for workers to address deeply rooted challenges.

At Fairtrade we prioritize workers' rights and strive to improve their quality of life, countering prevalent issues like exploitation and substandard working conditions.

By helping create a market that prioritizes ethical sourcing, having strong standards, and partnering with labor organizations across the world, Fairtrade supports workers.

Because workers deserve better wages, safe working conditions, and the ability to plan ahead and invest in their communities. It is simply fair.

How Fairtrade addresses workers’ rights

Strong standards at the core

Fairtrade standards are based on the conventions and recommendations of the International Labour Organization (ILO). We also include rules on health and safety while also establishing employment conditions for topics like wages, leave time, social security, and contracts in our Hired Labor Standard.

Workers with a voice

Fairtrade requires employers to recognize the right of their employees to participate in a union to negotiate better wages and working conditions with their employers. We also organize training activities to teach workers their rights and how to engage with management.

Fair prices

Good infrastructure, working conditions, and paying fair wages starts with getting a fair price for your product. Fairtrade stipulates a minimum price for products which acts as a safety net for farmers and workers. The Fairtrade Minimum Price is the minimum that producer organizations are paid when selling their products through Fairtrade.

Fair wages

Fairtrade Standards state that employers must pay at least national minimum wage and move towards a living wage. A living wage is a human right and Fairtrade’s ambition is that every worker can earn a living wage and enjoy decent working conditions.

Working with labor organizations

Fairtrade partners with labour rights and other organizations to make broad changes that improve the lives of all workers, not just those employed by Fairtrade certified farms and plantations. Our initiatives are meant to bring others along and drive change beyond the individual company level.

Community development

Part of the financial Fairtrade Premium has to be invested in community projects such as education and healthcare, uplifting the living standards of workers and their families.

Responsible businesses

Choosing Fairtrade means supporting standards and actions that put more power in the hands of workers themselves.
