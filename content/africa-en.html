A fairer future

Fairtrade Africa works towards fairer terms of trade for farmers and workers, safe working conditions, environmental protection, and quality. This approach prioritises the lives of farmers and workers in supply chains and makes them active participants in the decision-making process.

Areas of focus

We want to build a world where everyone benefits from quality products, and enjoys quality of life. But this cannot be done in silo. You cannot separate human rights from environmental rights, child protection from living incomes and gender equity from worker’s rights.
