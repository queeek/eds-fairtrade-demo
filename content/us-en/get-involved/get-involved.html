Get Involved

Fairtrade is more than a certification - it’s a movement. A movement of people who believe trade should never exploit the farmers and workers who feed us. A movement built on fairness, equality and human rights. A movement powered by your voice.

Join the Movement

Buy Fairtrade

The #1 way to get involved in Fairtrade is to buy certified products. In doing this, you are creating demand for goods that were purchased on fair trade terms and telling companies that sustainable and ethical sourcing is important to you.

If you appreciate the work that we do, show us by choosing Fairtrade alternatives to the goods that you use every day. Make it part of your daily life just like you would by bringing reusable bags to the grocery store or asking for a beverage without a straw.

Donate

The individuals who grow and pick the food that eventually lands on our tables often struggle to put food on their own. The current trade system too often leaves farmers without the resources or protections they deserve.

Together, we can shift this. Fairtrade doesn’t just certify products, we also run international programming to uplift smallholder farmer communities by educating on gender equality, enacting preventative programming to reduce child labor, funding agricultural resilience programs and supporting farmers in responding to climate crises.

Your support helps bring a fairer deal to more farmers and workers. When you donate to Fairtrade, you are supporting a movement that builds a future where farmers and workers can earn a secure, sustainable living and invest confidently in what comes next.

"A donation from you today can help bring Fairtrade to more small farmers all over the world, so they can earn a fair price for their produce and invest in their futures."

Rosine, a cocoa farmer in Côte d’Ivoire
